Sidequest Feed · Hong Kong 🇭🇰

Step outside your comfort zone.

Etie pairs visitors with HK locals to tackle real-world micro-challenges rather than standard itineraries. Every sidequest is a duo mission, not a tour.

WHAT IS A SIDEQUEST?

Shared interest + comfort-zone constraint. e.g., playing football while attempting 3 new skill moves, or dining by letting the local order mystery dishes blind. You pick the interest, you pick the constraint — your match agrees to do it with you.
